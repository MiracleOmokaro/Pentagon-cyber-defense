Attack vectors by region

North America

Identity compromise, phishing, ransomware, public-app exploits

Highest share of investigated high-value incidents in several reports

Europe

Public-facing app exploits, malware, identity abuse

Mix of financially motivated crime and geopolitical activity

Asia-Pacific

Public apps, supply chain, cloud & IoT, espionage playbooks

Manufacturing concentration drives both crime and state-aligned activity

Middle East & Africa

Malware, living-off-the-land, exposed VPNs/firewalls, ransomware

Higher rates of opportunistic attacks on exposed infrastructure

Latin America

Ransomware and opportunistic volume

Often among the highest weekly attacks per organisation

Identity and phishing are nearly universal. Exploitation of public-facing applications and exposed infrastructure is especially common in Europe, Asia-Pacific, and parts of Africa and the Middle East.
