About

A lean, founder-operated cybersecurity services company.

Pentagon Cyber Defense is built by a focused team of five. We have no employees — only the founding team and carefully selected independent operators when a client needs them. We are building a real company, structured for registration and growth, not a costume of a firm.

What we are

We help any business — from personal websites to startups and growing companies — protect their systems through private, controlled security assessments and clear, prioritised fix plans. Proper security should be an early-stage expense you can actually pay, not a choice between high cost and an unreliable stranger.

What we are not

Precision over costume.

Not a global firm

We do not rent a skyline photo and call it an office. No fake headcount. No “worldwide presence” that is actually one laptop.

Not a public bug bounty

We do not list your systems for strangers to attack. Work is private, scoped, and covered by an NDA before sensitive detail is shared.

Not an enterprise programme

We do not sell a 200-page catalogue. Price is fitted to what you actually run, as a startup expense, not a department budget.

Not a student project in disguise

We are building a real company — structured for registration and growth. We also do not hide behind student status.

Who does the work

Five founders. No employees.

The company is the founding team. When a client needs extra hands, we bring in carefully selected independent operators — people who can actually find and explain holes — not a bench of names on a slide.

How we execute

Principles

Do fewer things

One primary offer: private assessment plus a clear fix plan. Everything else supports that.

Quality over roster size

Better six people who can actually find and explain holes than twenty who cannot.

Proof, not claims

Every engagement must produce a report a client would be willing to show someone else.

Be precise about who we are

Founder-operated. No employees. No pretence of being a global firm. No hiding behind student status either.

Operating rules

  1. 01Speed of delivery matters almost as much as quality.
  2. 02Never overpromise.
  3. 03Never hide who we are. Never pretend to be a big firm.
  4. 04Every week: a client conversation, a completed assessment, or a measurable skill gain.
  5. 05Kill anything that does not lead to better delivery or more proof.

Around the core offer

Awareness & coordination

Many owners only have a vague sense that something could go wrong. We explain the real risk in plain language and help coordinate the work — the local relationship layer, not a public hunt.

Skill pipeline

We develop practical ability in people who can actually find and explain holes, then put only those operators on client work. Certificates are not the proof. Output is.
